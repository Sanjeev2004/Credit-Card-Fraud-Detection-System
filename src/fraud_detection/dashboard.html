Read the results correctly

Precision tells you how many alerts were actually fraud. Recall tells you how much of the labeled fraud the model caught.

Average precision (AP) summarizes the precision–recall curve across thresholds. Higher is better.

Fraud is rare. A model that always predicts “legitimate” can have very high accuracy while catching no fraud.

The examples are deliberately balanced for demonstration; they are not a representative traffic sample. Scores are uncalibrated. Results come from a seeded random split of a historical dataset and do not establish future payment performance.
